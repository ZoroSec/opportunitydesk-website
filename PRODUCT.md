# OpportunityDesk

OpportunityDesk helps business brokers, real estate professionals, and acquisition teams find relevant businesses and owners and open an initial conversation.

The current focus is baby-boomer-owned businesses and potential ownership transition. Identification does not establish that a business is for sale. Other searches can include customers, suppliers, and specialist service providers.

Visitors define geography, industry, role, and objective in a brief. The website downloads a text file locally. It does not submit an enquiry or start outreach; no enquiry address is configured.
